# Screen Space Shaders ice attribution

The seasonal ice shaders and ice normal/crack textures are adapted from **Screen Space Shaders** by **Ascii1457**, ICE VERSION module (introduced in Update 13), as supplied in the Update 23.5 download.

Original project: https://www.moddb.com/mods/stalker-anomaly/addons/screen-space-shaders

Author: https://www.moddb.com/members/ascii1457

Adaptation: unique resource names, seasonal runtime selection, compatibility with the existing Anthology shader interfaces, and restoration of the modpack's original water materials outside winter. The full SSS game material library is not replaced. Original authorship/comments are retained in the shader sources. Other SSS components already present in the engine/modpack retain their respective credits.

This attribution applies to `anthology_ice*.h` and `textures/anthology_seasons/ice_normal.dds`, `ice_cracks.dds`. NVIDIA DLSS and the IX-Ray FidelityFX DX11 integration have their separate licenses in the player package.
